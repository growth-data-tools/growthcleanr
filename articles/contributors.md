## Funding and organizations

The current release of growthcleanr was funded by the HHS Office of the
Secretary Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Trust Fund (OS-PCORTF) and
supported by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Division of
Nutrition, Physical Activity, and Obesity. Original development of
growthcleanr was supported in large part by the American Academy of
Pediatrics (AAP) Pediatric Research in Office Settings (PROS) network,
including with data provided by the AAP PROS Comparative Effectiveness
Research through Collaborative Electronic Reporting (CER²) electronic
health record data network. The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia
contributed data and programming support. The University of Manitoba and
Penn State College of Medicine provided salary support, and Penn State
Health Golisano Children’s Hospital contributed data.
